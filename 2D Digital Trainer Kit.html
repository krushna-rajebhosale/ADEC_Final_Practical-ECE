👋 Step 1: drag a chip from the shelf into a socket (or tap a chip). Step 2: tap one pin, then another pin, to join them with a wire. Step 3: flip the switches and watch the lights.

Analog practical:
Tap a wire to remove it. Drag a chip off the board to remove it. Tap the small CA/CC/BCD label on a display to change its type. Unconnected inputs count as HIGH (1), like real TTL. Pinouts are written from memory – please check them against your datasheet.
